## 4. Methodological Limitations

- Multiple prices within a group do not establish conditional positivity.
- Extreme price differences should not automatically be treated as errors.
- Product-description inconsistencies may affect treatment interpretation.
- Further sensitivity analysis is required before causal conclusions.

## 5. Conclusion

Product-country-month grouping identifies meaningful price variation
for 54.83% of transactions. However, conditional treatment overlap
remains a preliminary diagnostic and requires further validation.
